Welcome! Three quick steps

  1. Paste your CV text in Settings and click Save CV.
  2. Optional but recommended: add a free Gemini key (aistudio.google.com/apikey). It turns on job matches and tailored resumes.
  3. Pick your days and time, switch Daily updates on, then click Update now.

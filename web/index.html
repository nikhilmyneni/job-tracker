Job Hunter

Your job search, tracked for you

Job Hunter reads your Gmail (read-only), keeps every application up to date, finds jobs that fit your CV, and writes a tailored one-page resume for each one. It's free.

  • Private. Runs in your browser. Your data stays in a hidden app folder in your own Google Drive, and nobody else, including whoever runs this site, can see it.
  • Read-only. It never sends, deletes or changes your email.
  • Free AI. Optional: add your own free Gemini, Groq or OpenRouter key for job matching and resumes.
